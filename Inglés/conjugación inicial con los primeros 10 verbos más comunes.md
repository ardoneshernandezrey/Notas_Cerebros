Perfecto, Ardon. Aquí tienes un cuadro de conjugación inicial con los primeros 10 verbos más comunes, en los cuatro tiempos básicos que definimos (presente simple, pasado simple, futuro simple, presente continuo).
---
📘 Conjugación de los 10 verbos iniciales
Verbo	Presente Simple	Pasado Simple	Futuro Simple	Presente Continuo
be	I am	I was	I will be	I am being
have	I have	I had	I will have	I am having
do	I do	I did	I will do	I am doing
say	I say	I said	I will say	I am saying
go	I go	I went	I will go	I am going
get	I get	I got	I will get	I am getting
make	I make	I made	I will make	I am making
know	I know	I knew	I will know	I am knowing
think	I think	I thought	I will think	I am thinking
take	I take	I took	I will take	I am taking
---
📌 Cómo practicar
Repite cada fila en voz alta tres veces.
Haz una frase corta con cada tiempo:
I eat pizza → presente simple.
I ate pizza yesterday → pasado simple.
I will eat pizza tomorrow → futuro simple.
I am eating pizza now → presente continuo.
Aplica la misma lógica a estos 10 verbos.
